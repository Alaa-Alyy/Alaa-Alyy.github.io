Multi-Agent AI Home Design & Renovation Assistant

An orchestrated AI system that transforms a room photo, user preferences, and a fixed EGP budget into a personalized, purchase-ready home redesign.

4 AI AGENTSVision • Design • Shopping • Budget
1 ORCHESTRATORLangGraph-based deterministic workflow
ITERATIVE LOOPDesign ↔ Budget Optimization
EGP-AWAREReal products • Real prices • Purchase links
  • Vision Agent analyzes room images and extracts room type, furniture, structural elements, lighting, colors, and spatial information.
  • Design Agent uses Llama via Groq to generate personalized furniture, decor, layout, color, and lighting recommendations.
  • Shopping Agent performs semantic product retrieval using embeddings, PostgreSQL, and pgvector to find relevant products from Egyptian catalogs.
  • Budget Agent calculates itemized costs, detects budget overruns, and identifies expensive items.
  • Iterative Design–Budget loop automatically revises over-budget designs by replacing expensive products or removing non-essential items.
  • Shared structured state enables all agents to communicate and maintain consistency throughout the workflow.
  • Generates before/after room previews, ranked product recommendations, itemized budgets, and direct purchase links.
