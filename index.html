ELSEWHERE — Earth Analogs for Moon & Mars

Our NASA Space Apps 2026 entry. Pick a candidate Moon or Mars base site and ELSEWHERE finds the places on Earth whose terrain looks most like it, so teams can test there first. Every match comes with the reasons behind it and a short mission decision brief.

1.14MEarth windows searched
20 KMterrain windows
3Dglobes for Earth, Moon & Mars
  • Python terrain pipeline on NASA SRTM, LOLA and MOLA elevation data: slope, relief and height variation for every 20 km window.
  • Explainable nearest-neighbour search over 1,143,707 Earth windows, so each result shows which features made it a match.
  • FastAPI backend with JSON-Schema contracts and an LLM chat grounded in the search results (OpenAI / Gemini).
  • React + Vite front end with CesiumJS and three.js globes.
  • Tested with pytest and Vitest; shipped with Docker on Railway. Team of 3, main contributor.

Roomly AI — Multi-Agent Home Designer

Tell it about your room in Egyptian Arabic or English, give it a budget in EGP, and it picks real products from Egyptian stores and shows them inside a photo of your own room.

12agents in one pipeline
EGPreal stores, real prices
3AI providers with fallback
  • Conversational intake in Egyptian Arabic and English, turned into structured JSON the other agents use.
  • RAG over design knowledge with bge-m3 embeddings.
  • Searches IKEA Egypt and Amazon.eg, filters by price, relevance and stock, checks every link is live, and ranks the picks within the budget.
  • Edits the user's own room photo with FLUX.2 using the chosen products as references, then a vision model checks the items really appear.
  • A router falls back between Groq, Hugging Face and Cloudflare Workers AI when a free tier runs out; the user approves the plan before anything is generated.
